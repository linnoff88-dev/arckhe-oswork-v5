Who it’s for

Made for your moment

is for you if

  • You coordinate a team and processes and you want ready-to-use material, without rewriting everything.
  • You prepare lessons and follow-up feedback and you want to save time without losing your way of writing.
  • You already have a career built and you want AI to fit your way of working.
  • You use an AI chat now and then and you want to stop improvising with every request.
  • You’ve never programmed and you’d rather learn by doing, at your own pace.

not for you if

  • You look for advanced technical content on how tools work from the inside.
  • You want to watch video lessons and take notes, instead of building your own pieces.

What you get out of

Four pieces, assembled by you

Folders by context — one place only, in front of your work, with the material the AI needs to read before it answers.

Orientation sheet — your rules written once: the tone, what not to make up, how to sign.

Request template — the task you repeat the most in your month, saved ready, with blanks to swap.

Checking routine — what you delegate, what you check, and what you don’t hand to anyone.
